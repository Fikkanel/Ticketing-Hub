## Sprint Ceremonies

### Daily Standup
- **Time**: 09:00 WIB
- **Duration**: 15 minutes
- **Format**: What did I do? What will I do? Any blockers?

### Sprint Planning
- **When**: Day 1 of Sprint
- **Duration**: 2 hours
- **Output**: Sprint backlog

### Sprint Review
- **When**: Last day of Sprint
- **Duration**: 1 hour
- **Output**: Demo & feedback

### Sprint Retrospective
- **When**: After Sprint Review
- **Duration**: 1 hour
- **Output**: Action items for improvement
